Web Design Projects

PROJECT 1

This Is Nima is a straightforward portfolio site for Niamh “Nima” Martin, a makeup artist with years of experience in bridal and occasion work. The goal was to create a clean, modern, sophisticated website that effectively showcased her work. Niamh wanted to focus more on her imagery than on the text, so we utilized flip boxes and drop-downs where possible, and numerous sliders to display her work.

Live website

PROJECT 2

Run The Night Series came wanting a landing page for their main website/brand, along with 3 sub sites for their series of races across Ireland and in Luxembourg. We cloned the design of the sub sites to keep everything cohesive, but added uniqueness with each location having its own main colour.

Live website

PROJECT 3

The 1st Cork Crosshaven Sea Scouts wanted a website that was big, colourful and dynamic. As well as this, given their target audience we wanted to keep things friendly and inviting. Having come with an already established logo, we stuck to those colours and font style when creating the website, to keep a cohesive look throughout.
